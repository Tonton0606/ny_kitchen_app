> Source of Truth: PROJECT_BLUEPRINT.md Section 7.8

---

## Purpose
Document the reporting features available to Administrators.

---

## Report Types

### Sales Reports
- Daily sales report
- Weekly sales report
- Monthly sales report
- Annual sales report

### Business Reports
- Best-selling food items
- Best-selling categories
- Cashier performance report

### Security Reports
- Login summary (successful, failed)
- Failed login attempts
- Security events

## Report Features
- Preview report in application.
- Print report.
- Export as PDF.
- Export as CSV.

## Report Charts (fl_chart - Proposed)
- Revenue trend chart
- Best-selling items chart
- Payment method breakdown

---

## Access
- Reports are accessible to Admin only.
- Cashier has no access to reports module.
