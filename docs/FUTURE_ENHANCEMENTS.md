# docs/FUTURE_ENHANCEMENTS.md
# N.Y Kitchen — Future Enhancements

> These features are intentionally excluded from the initial version.
> See: PROJECT_BLUEPRINT.md Section 20

---

## Purpose
Document features that may be added in future versions after the initial release.

---

## Approved Future Enhancements

### Cloud Features (Supabase)
- Cloud backup of SQLite database
- Cross-device synchronization
- Remote Admin access
- Authentication support via Supabase Auth
- Future analytics

### Receipt Enhancements
- Save receipt as PDF to local storage
- Email receipt (future)

### Reporting Enhancements
- Advanced analytics and charts
- Custom date range reports

### Payment Enhancements
- Additional payment methods
- Integration with payment terminals

---

## Permanently Excluded (Initial Version)
- Inventory Management
- Supplier Management
- Customer Accounts / Loyalty Programs
- Delivery Tracking
- Online Ordering
- Multi-branch Support
- Employee Payroll
- Accounting System

---

## Rules
- Future enhancements must be documented in PROJECT_BLUEPRINT.md before implementation.
- No future enhancement may be implemented without explicit approval.
- Excluded features must never be introduced as a "small addition."
